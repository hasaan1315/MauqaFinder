IMPORTANT NOTICE

PPSC has introduced the feature of Online Fee Collection. Applicants are advised to deposit fees online by using ATMs, Mobile Phone Banking, Internet Banking, Jazz Cash, Easy Paisa, U Paisa, other available microfinance banks or over-the-counter (by visiting the nearest 1Link Member Banks Branches). Fee deposited on old Challan Form (32-A) of PPSC, Bank Draft, Pay Order and Cheque is not acceptable.

You are advised to view the following instructions / guidelines before starting the process of online application submission.
